Questions schools ask

Good questions.

Do parents need another app?

No. SchoolKey replaces the other apps. Students, parents, teachers and leaders all use one app, and parents with several children see them all in one account.

Where is student data stored?

Inside the UAE. We never sell student data, never show ads, and never use it to train outside AI models.

What happens to records when a student changes school?

They stay in the student's Learning Passport, owned by the family. With the parent's approval, the new school receives them on day one.

Which curricula do you support?

British, American, IB, Indian and Ministry of Education curricula, each with its own grading scale and report card, in Arabic and English.

How long does it take to start?

About two weeks. We import your students, classes and timetables for free, and you can run SchoolKey beside your current system for a term.

Is UAE Pass sign-in available now?

UAE Pass sign-in arrives once SchoolKey is approved as a UAE Pass service provider. Until then, pilot schools use a secure email login.
